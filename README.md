# Anusha Raman
I am curious about how things work, why people do things the way they do, and how we can make those experiences better.

Having spent much of my career building and scaling technology products, I have learned that building something just because the technology makes it possible isn't enough. A product needs a reason to exist — it should solve a real problem, create meaningful value for the people it's built for, and make their lives easier.

I believe technology should fit naturally into people's lives, not the other way around. Users shouldn't have to change how they think, work, or go about their day just to use a product. The best products understand their users and adapt to the way they already do things.

That's the lens through which I explore new ideas. I am interested in what's possible, but even more interested in what's useful — moving beyond the hype, questioning assumptions, and figuring out how emerging technology can solve real problems in intuitive ways.

This space is where I experiment with ideas, build prototypes, explore new technologies, and share what I learn along the way. Some explorations may turn into products; others may simply lead to better questions. Both are worthwhile.

I am always happy to connect with people who challenge conventional thinking, care about the problems they are solving, and believe that the best technology doesn't just impress people — it makes a meaningful difference to them.
